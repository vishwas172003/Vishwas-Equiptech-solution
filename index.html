How a project runs

The same five steps whether it is one new service bay or a full factory air network.

  1. 1

    Site visit

    We look at the site and any existing lines, check for leaks and pressure drop, and note where air is needed.

  2. 2

    Layout and quote

    You get a proposed piping layout with drop points and a clear quotation.

  3. 3

    Installation

    Our team installs pipes, supports, FRL units and connections with neat, secure routing.

  4. 4

    Testing and handover

    Lines are leak tested and the system is commissioned before we hand it over.

  5. 5

    AMC and support

    Annual maintenance and service calls keep the system running.
